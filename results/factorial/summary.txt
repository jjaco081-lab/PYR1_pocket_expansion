CONCLUSION
  ZERO transitions in any cell, in either direction, with or without ligand.
  Occupancy changes nothing measurable about the conformation on this
  timescale. That is not a null result about the biology -- it is a
  restatement of 24's trapping: the observable is the stability of whichever
  state you start in. Ligand-dependence of the open/closed EQUILIBRIUM is
  invisible to unbiased MD and needs the PMF (60).
