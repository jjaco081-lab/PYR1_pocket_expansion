   The obvious story is 'the recovered ones clash and K59R does not'. It is
   false: V81I clashes LESS than K59R (0.12 vs 0.21 A) and was still
   recovered at rank 150. Ordered by clash:
     F108A  overlap 2.78 A  dVol -101 A^3  charge +0->+0  §32 rank 5
     F159L  overlap 1.42 A  dVol  -23 A^3  charge +0->+0  §32 rank 75
     K59R  overlap 0.21 A  dVol   +5 A^3  charge +1->+1  §32 rank 3907
     V81I  overlap 0.12 A  dVol  +27 A^3  charge +0->+0  §32 rank 150

   The axis that DOES separate them is VOLUME, and it separates cleanly:
     F108A  -101 A^3   relieves a 2.78 A clash        rank    5
     V81I    +27 A^3   fills a void (the GROW half)   rank  150
     F159L   -23 A^3   relieves a 1.42 A clash        rank   75
     K59R     +5 A^3   changes nothing about shape    rank 3907

   K59R is near-ISOSTERIC (+5 A^3) AND charge-neutral (Lys +1 -> Arg +1).
   What it changes is hydrogen-bond GEOMETRY: Arg's guanidinium is planar
   and bidentate where Lys's ammonium is not, and the crystal makes two
   bonds with it (NE-O2 2.64 A, NH1-O2 3.26 A, §33a). That is invisible to
   a volume term, invisible to a formal-charge term, and invisible to a
   clash term -- three separate blindnesses, which is why §32, §33 and the
   coupled-moves run in §36 all missed the same residue.

   So the signature is not hydrophobicity, not burial, not contact count.
   It is: DOES THE SUBSTITUTION MOVE VOLUME? If yes, a shape method finds
   it. If no, nothing we have built can see it.

================================================================================
5. THE DESIGN QUESTION: which positions can be FIXED, and which must stay
   ligand-specific?
================================================================================
   A position whose residue choice does not depend on the ligand can be
   set once and contributes a menu of 1. A position whose choice DOES
   depend on the ligand has to carry depth, and depth is what the library
   budget buys. So the design-relevant statistic is not how concentrated a
   position is, it is how much of that concentration survives conditioning
   on the ligand.

   Mutual information I(ligand ; residue) at each position, against a null
   that permutes residues among the clones at that position -- which holds
   both the residue distribution and the clone-per-ligand counts fixed, so
   the null has the same bias from small samples as the observation.
