VERDICT
==============================================================================
  The 250 ps ensemble is NOT converged. The WT-ABA reference moved +7.75
  kcal/mol between 50 and 250 ps and is still moving +2.62 within the 250 ps
  window, in the same direction. It has not settled, it is still climbing.

  Consequences, kept separate because they are not equally damaged:
    SIGN-based conclusions are void. The 50 ps 'K59 flip' was produced by
      an unconverged WT-ABA reference, not by the K59R substitution. At
      250 ps the same test gives the opposite answer.
    RANK-based conclusions survive a uniform reference error, since a
      constant added to every ddG in an arm cannot reorder them. They are
      still limited by the per-variant drift, which at a median 1.05
      kcal/mol is the same size as the spacing at the top of the ranking.
